# DecideLater — A Decision Assistant

DecideLater is a React-based web application designed to reduce daily decision fatigue. By analyzing competing choices against dynamic, weighted criteria, the application provides clear recommendations alongside transparent trade-off analysis.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Making dozens of small, daily decisions—from prioritizing tasks to choosing purchases—consumes mental energy and leads to decision fatigue. This often results in procrastination or suboptimal choices on important matters. DecideLater streamlines this process by converting subjective choices into objective, structured evaluations.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Context-Based Decision Frameworks:** Pre-configured evaluation templates tailored for specific decision categories:
  * **Financial:** Balances *Price*, *Frequency of Use*, and *Long-term Value*.
  * **Task & Productivity:** Balances *Urgency*, *Effort*, and *Impact*.
  * **Lifestyle & Leisure:** Balances *Time*, *Cost*, and *Personal Satisfaction*.
* **Transparent Weighted Scoring:** Automatically calculates scores based on user-defined priority sliders.
* **The "Regret & Trade-Off Check":** Highlights key trade-offs between the top recommendation and secondary options before finalizing a choice.
* **Decision History & Reflection Log:** Saves past decisions to allow users to review and rate the outcomes of their choices over time.
